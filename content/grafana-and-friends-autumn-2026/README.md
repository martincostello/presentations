# Grafana & Friends London: Autumn 2026

Slides for my presentation at [Grafana & Friends London: Autumn 2026][grafana-and-friends],
_"[Making Your First Open Source Contribution][session]"_.

## Abstract

> A lot of people are enthusiastic to contribute to an open source project, but
> can feel intimidated and overwhelmed by where to start and how to propose a change.
>
> In this talk I'll offer tips from 13 years of experience contributing to open
> source projects on what makes a good first contribution (spoilers: not just a
> new feature!) and how to structure your first PR to maximise the chances of it
> being accepted, particularly in this new age of AI agents.
>
> Maybe this could be the first step on your open source journey?
